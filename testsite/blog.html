Five tips for your first camping trip

By Sam Rivers, 3 March 2026

A first trip goes best when you keep it short, close to home and well planned. These are the tips our staff give every new camper.

    Pack warm layers, even in summer.
    Bring a headlamp and spare batteries.
    Pitch your tent before dark.
